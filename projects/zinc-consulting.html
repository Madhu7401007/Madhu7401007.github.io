Zinc VC — Investment Readiness Framework

A 5-stage, traffic-light framework for de-risking early-stage impact ventures — benchmarked against four industry frameworks and stress-tested on real cases including Tesla.

RoleLead Consultant
OrganisationZinc VC (via Imperial Business School)
TimeframeJun 2025 – Aug 2025
SectorVenture / Impact Investing

The Challenge

Zinc backs early-stage impact ventures, but existing assessment tools — IMP, DELTA, IRIS+, Nesta's Standards of Evidence — were built for later-stage companies: too data-heavy for pre-revenue ventures, blind to how readiness changes by stage, with no simple way to compare ventures at a glance.

The brief: design a practical framework that could spot impact risk early, before scale masks it, and give founders, funders and support teams a shared, stage-sensitive language for venture readiness.

The Approach

We designed a 5-stage framework — Intention, Experimentation, Validation, Inflection, Scaling — scoring 9 dimensions at every stage (What, Who, Scale, Duration, Additionality, Depth, Risk, Theory of Change, Measurability) with a traffic-light score, giving investors a quick shorthand for where a venture stands. A supplementary risk register mapping 10 risk categories points investors at what's actually blocking progress, not just what's wrong.

Benchmarked against IMP, DELTA, IRIS+ and Nesta across six criteria, it was the only framework to score well on stage-progression, early-stage usability, simplicity and visual clarity together.

To stress-test it, we ran real ventures through all 5 stages — including Tesla, which scored Amber/Red on Risk throughout (cobalt-mining exposure, unresolved battery-disposal impact, mission dilution by Stage 5) — proving even large, successful companies carry real risk. We pressure-tested the criteria with 10+ portfolio companies and 20+ stakeholders.
