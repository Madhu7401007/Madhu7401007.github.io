Presentation QA Add-in

An LLM-powered QA tool built from an AI use case identified during a client strategy engagement, shipped end-to-end in a two-week sprint. It turned into a €270K follow-on engagement.

The Challenge

Mid-way through a six-week strategy engagement, I kept seeing the same bottleneck: senior consultants losing hours manually checking that every number, chart and claim in a client-facing deck matched the underlying source data before it went out the door. It's the kind of quiet, repetitive risk that doesn't show up on a project plan but eats real time on every single deliverable, and one slip in front of a client is expensive.

The specifics of the client engagement are confidential, but the underlying problem is a common one in fast-moving advisory work: quality control that scales linearly with headcount instead of getting easier over time.

The Approach

The use case came out of that same strategy engagement. We flagged it to the client alongside the rest of our recommendations, and rather than leave it as a slide in a deck, they asked for it to be built. I scoped the tool, designed the QA logic, and shipped an end-to-end LLM-powered add-in in a two-week sprint, from first line of code to something the team was actually using day to day.

Identify the use case
Scope the QA logic
Build (Python, FastAPI, LLM)
Ship in 2 weeks

The tool uses an LLM to cross-check figures, formatting and claims within a presentation against source data and flag discrepancies automatically, turning a manual, hours-long review into a fast, repeatable check.
