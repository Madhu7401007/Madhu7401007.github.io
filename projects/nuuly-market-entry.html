Nuuly — Strategic Market Entry into Turkey

A full market-entry strategy for Urban Outfitters' clothing-rental platform, sized bottom-up, benchmarked against two direct competitors, and shaped into a phased go-to-market plan.

The Challenge

Nuuly's apparel-rental model has grown quickly in the US, but staying purely domestic and rental-only caps its long-term growth. The strategic question we were set: should Nuuly expand internationally, and if so, where and how, without diluting the sustainable, circular-fashion positioning that differentiates it?

The Approach

We sized the Turkish apparel resale and rental market bottom-up, separately for Nuuly's Rent and Thrift lines, building out TAM, SAM and SOM for each, then projected market share growth over Nuuly's first five years of entry.

We then benchmarked Nuuly against two direct competitors already operating in Turkey, DCEY, a local rental incumbent, and Trendyol, an Alibaba-backed multi-sided marketplace, using a Blue Ocean Strategy Canvas to map where Nuuly should compete differently rather than head-on: eliminating the overconsumption and narrow demographic focus of incumbents, reducing price-per-item and environmental impact, and creating a casual rental category neither competitor offers.

From there we built a phased entry recommendation using a strategy cascade, a direct-to-consumer model backed by strategic partnerships, and mapped how incumbents were likely to respond once they felt Nuuly's entry, so the recommendation accounted for competitive reaction, not just a static market snapshot.
