The Challenge

Every technology due diligence engagement starts the same way: a target company's data scattered across different systems, formats and stakeholders — engineering documentation in one place, infrastructure inventories in another, product roadmaps living in someone's inbox. Across SaaS, ERP and digital-platform targets, deal teams needed a fast, reliable read on a target's technology maturity, and raw diligence data doesn't give you that on its own.

The Approach

I worked across 5+ private equity technology due diligences — €100M+ of combined deal value — structuring ambiguous, inconsistent source material into a consistent, executive-ready format every time. The goal wasn't just cleaning data; it was building a repeatable way to turn "here's everything the target sent us" into "here's what the deal team needs to know."

That meant working directly with senior stakeholders to understand what each investment committee actually needed to see, then applying the same structuring logic consistently across engagements — regardless of how messy or inconsistent the underlying target's own systems were.
